## Phase Goal

**As a** job seeker, **I want to** create, find, review and move my applications through their states, **so that** I have one reliable record of every selection process.

This plan carries tags end to end (shared normalization in contracts, stored key, `GET /api/tags`) and turns the tracer's list items into the DS cards.

<objective>
Make tags trustworthy and visible: one pure normalization in `@tfm/contracts` used by the service as the source of truth (D-09, APP-12), a stored per-tag `key` that later filters and aggregates use, and `GET /api/tags` with counts (D-05, APP-14). Then render each application as the DS card with StatusBadge, TagChip, salary and last-updated date (APP-04, D-14, D-17).

Purpose: filters (plans 02-04 to 02-06) and the form (plan 02-11) depend on one definition of "the same tag"; the cards are the first thing the user reads.
Output: `tag.normalize.ts` + tests; repository/service/mapper changes; `tags/tags.routes.ts` + tests; common labels, formatter, StatusBadge, TagChip + tests; card VM/mapper/component.
</objective>
