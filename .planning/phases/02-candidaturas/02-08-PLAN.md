## Phase Goal

**As a** job seeker, **I want to** create, find, review and move my applications through their states, **so that** I have one reliable record of every selection process.

This plan completes the form: conditions, tracking and tags by group with normalization feedback.

<objective>
Add the remaining sections of the shared form (D-16): «Condiciones» and «Seguimiento» (with «Estado inicial» only in create, D-07) and «Etiquetas» with one input per group that reuses `normalizeTags` from contracts for immediate feedback (D-09, APP-12). The VM, mapper and Spanish messages already exist from plan 02-07; this plan adds presentation and interaction.

Purpose: a complete record needs conditions, tracking data and tags; tags feed the stack filter.
Output: textarea primitive, ConditionsSection, TrackingSection, TagsSection, TagGroupInput and tests.
</objective>
