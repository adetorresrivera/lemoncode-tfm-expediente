## Phase Goal

**As a** job seeker, **I want to** create, find, review and move my applications through their states, **so that** I have one reliable record of every selection process.

This plan completes the filter panel: status, work mode, seniority, sort, the mobile disclosure, and the stack filter fed by `GET /api/tags`.

<objective>
Give the user every filter of APP-06 and the sort control of APP-08 inside the panel built in plan 02-05, and the stack tag picker sourced from `GET /api/tags` with only `stack` items (D-05, APP-14), with its loading, error and empty states. Fix the Phase 1 primitive styles the new controls inherit (UI-SPEC C-3, C-4).

Purpose: the demo ends with «see it filtered»; status and stack are the filters a recruiter conversation needs.
Output: native-select primitive, restyled button/toggle/toggle-group, RequestStatus, status/select/tag filter components, stack tags hook and tests.
</objective>
