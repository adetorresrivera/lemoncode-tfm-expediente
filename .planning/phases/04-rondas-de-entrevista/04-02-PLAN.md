## Phase Goal

**As a** job seeker, **I want to** log the interview rounds of an application, **so that** its process history and status stay in sync without extra bookkeeping.

This plan completes what a round records and what the list shows: the dialog gains Fecha y hora, Modalidad, Resultado and Notas (and moves to its own file), and each row shows the date, the mode, the outcome badge and the notes, in the D-07 order.

<objective>
On the path built by 04-01: move `InterviewDialog` to `components/interview-dialog.tsx` and add the four remaining controls with the datetime-local ↔ ISO UTC conversion (Task 1); then render each round with `InterviewItem` and `InterviewOutcomeBadge`, sorted and labelled by the mapper, with `formatDateTime` (Task 2). No API change: `POST /interviews` already accepts and returns every field.

Purpose: after this plan the user records a complete round and reads it back at a glance (INT-01, INT-05).
Output: format helpers with fixed-zone tests; dialog, item and badge components; the new rounds test file.
</objective>
