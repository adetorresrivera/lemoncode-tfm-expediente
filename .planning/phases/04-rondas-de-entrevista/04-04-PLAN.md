## Phase Goal

**As a** job seeker, **I want to** log the interview rounds of an application, **so that** its process history and status stay in sync without extra bookkeeping.

This plan closes the phase: the status select also waits while a round is saving (the last part of D-16), the compose smoke proves the round endpoints in the built image, the architecture doc states their answers, and the whole suite passes.

<objective>
Task 1: give `StatusChange` a `locked` prop fed by `interviewActions.busy`, so the select is aria-disabled during any round mutation (the hook guard from 04-01 already drops the request). Task 2: add the round round-trip to `scripts/compose-smoke.sh`, update the endpoint table of `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, and run the phase gate.

Purpose: one change at a time is visible to keyboard and screen-reader users, and the demo step «añadir ronda» is proven end to end under `docker compose`.
Output: status select wiring and tests; extended compose smoke; doc rows; green gate.
</objective>

## Assumptions (explicit, for the user to review)

- The select keeps `aria-busy` for its own PATCH only; during a round mutation it is `aria-disabled` without `aria-busy`, because its value is not the thing being saved (UI-SPEC «Busy state»).
- The smoke reuses the application it already creates and moves to enviada; deleting that application at the end also removes its embedded rounds. The missing failure trap of the smoke is the open finding WR-02 and is not changed here.
- `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` is in Spanish like the rest of `docs/`.
