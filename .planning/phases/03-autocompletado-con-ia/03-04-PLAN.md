## Phase Goal

**As a** job seeker, **I want to** paste an offer and get a reviewable draft of the application form, **so that** I fill an application in seconds without losing anything I already typed.

This plan expands the tracer on the web side: the merge covers every scalar field, and the user sees which fields the AI filled («IA») and which relevant ones it could not («Complétalo tú»), with the lifecycle the user decided (D-11, D-13, D-16, D-17).

<objective>
Complete `mergeExtractionIntoForm` for salary and currency and compute `missingFields`; add the `AiMark` badge and the missing-field treatment to `FormField`/`controlProps` and the job and conditions sections; keep the marks as session state cleared by a form-level TanStack `listeners.onChange`.

Purpose: AI-07 and AI-08 make the draft reviewable; without them the user cannot tell AI values from their own.
Output: mapper rules with tests, `AiMark`, field and section changes, marks hook and listener, flow tests.
</objective>

## Assumptions (explicit, for the user to review)

- Currency rule (RESEARCH Pitfall 10 and Q7, accepted by the user on 2026-10-31): the UI-SPEC rule «currency is fillable when both salary fields are empty» is narrowed to «…and this analysis filled at least one salary field and returned a currency». A currency without any salary is not filled, not marked and not counted.
- Salary guard (Pitfall 9): when the user kept one salary value, the AI fill of the other one is skipped if it would leave the minimum above the maximum. This fills fewer fields, never overwrites one.
- Marks live in a `useAiMarks` hook in the pod (D-17 discretion: `useState` with `Set`s, stable callbacks); they are not stored in TanStack Form meta.
- The marks are cleared on a successful save before the scene navigates; on a failed save they stay (D-17).
- Spec-less edge probes AI-06, AI-07 and AI-08 came back unclassified («review manually»): flagged here as reviewed by hand. AI-06 (merge never overwrites, persists nothing) is covered by the 03-01 and this plan's merge truths; AI-07 by the badge lifecycle truths; AI-08 by the missing-field truths. No probe item is dismissed.
- AI-02 precision is lifted here for the web (String(n) into the form); the API part is in 03-03.
