## Phase Goal

**As a** job seeker, **I want to** paste an offer and get a reviewable draft of the application form, **so that** I fill an application in seconds without losing anything I already typed.

## Assumptions (explicit, for the user to review)

- «Filled at least one field or tag» is `merge.aiFields.length > 0 || merge.aiTags.length > 0`, the same condition under which the summary is not the nothing-new sentence. A re-analysis that only clears earlier «IA» values therefore keeps the panel open with «No hemos encontrado datos nuevos…».
- The focus rule runs in `OfferPanel` (it owns the DOM refs), in an effect after `open` goes from true to false. Radix unmounts the content when it closes, so focus that was on the AI button has fallen to `body` by then; both «lost» and «inside the content» lead to the trigger. A manual close from the trigger leaves focus on the trigger, so the rule is a no-op there.
- The trigger stays disabled while analysing (plan 03-08), so the panel cannot be closed by hand before the answer; the fold is the only programmatic close.
- Spec-less edge probes lifted here (gap scope: the panel's open/close and focus): AI-05 idempotency (reopen and analyse again folds again with the same merge rules) and AI-05 concurrency (answer arriving while the user types elsewhere; unmount during the wait stays covered by plan 03-08). Already covered by plans 03-01..03-09 and not touched: AI-01, AI-02, AI-03, AI-04 (plus 03-10 for tag grouping), AI-07, AI-09, and AI-06/AI-08 merge and highlight rules (03-04..03-06).
- UI-SPEC «UI Considerations» rows lifted: empty (nothing new → stays open), error (stays open), concurrency (cancel → stays open, focus to the AI button; double click unchanged), populated (re-analysis after reopening), long-running (backstop, carried from 03-09). The other rows (overflow, long-text, zero-one-many, currency default, AI chips) are unaffected by the fold.
