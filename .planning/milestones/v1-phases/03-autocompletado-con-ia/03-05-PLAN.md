## Phase Goal

**As a** job seeker, **I want to** paste an offer and get a reviewable draft of the application form, **so that** I fill an application in seconds without losing anything I already typed.

This plan makes the draft reviewable on the form itself: the user sees which fields the AI filled («IA») and which relevant ones it could not («Complétalo tú»), with the lifecycle the user decided (D-11, D-13, D-16, D-17). Plan 03-04 already computes both lists.

<objective>
Add the `AiMark` badge and the missing-field treatment to `FormField`/`controlProps` and the job and conditions sections; keep the marks as session state, set from each merge and cleared by a form-level TanStack `listeners.onChange` and by a successful save.

Purpose: AI-07 and AI-08 make the draft reviewable; without them the user cannot tell AI values from their own.
Output: `AiMark`, field and section changes with tests, marks hook and listener, flow tests.
</objective>

## Assumptions (explicit, for the user to review)

- The «IA» badge follows the merge rules of plan 03-04: a currency is marked only when the merge filled it (both salary fields empty and at least one filled), and a salary skipped by the inversion guard gets no badge.
- Marks live in a `useAiMarks` hook in the pod (D-17 discretion: `useState` with `Set`s, stable callbacks); they are not stored in TanStack Form meta.
- The marks are cleared on a successful save before the scene navigates; on a failed save they stay (D-17).
- Spec-less edge probes AI-07 and AI-08 came back unclassified («review manually»): flagged here as reviewed by hand. AI-07 is covered by the badge lifecycle truths; AI-08 by the highlight truths here and the missing-list truths of plan 03-04. No probe item is dismissed.
