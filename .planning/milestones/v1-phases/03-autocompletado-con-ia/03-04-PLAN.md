## Phase Goal

**As a** job seeker, **I want to** paste an offer and get a reviewable draft of the application form, **so that** I fill an application in seconds without losing anything I already typed.

This plan expands the tracer's merge on the web side: the draft now fills the salary range and the currency without ever inverting a range or inventing a currency, and the user is told which relevant fields the AI could not fill. Plan 03-05 then marks those fields on the form («IA», «Complétalo tú»).

<objective>
Complete `mergeExtractionIntoForm` for salary and currency and compute `missingFields`, with the merge's first dedicated unit-test file; carry the missing list in the success status and name it in the panel's summary.

Purpose: AI-06 for every scalar field, and the first, textual half of AI-08 (the field highlight is plan 03-05).
Output: mapper rules with unit tests, success status and summary sentence, flow tests.
</objective>

## Assumptions (explicit, for the user to review)

- Currency rule (RESEARCH Pitfall 10 and Q7, accepted by the user on 2026-10-31): the UI-SPEC rule «currency is fillable when both salary fields are empty» is narrowed to «…and this analysis filled at least one salary field and returned a currency». A currency without any salary is not filled, not marked and not counted.
- Salary guard (Pitfall 9): when the user kept one salary value, the AI fill of the other one is skipped if it would leave the minimum above the maximum. This fills fewer fields, never overwrites one.
- `AiMarks` is declared here, next to the two lists it mirrors, so plan 03-05 adds no VM change; nothing renders it in this plan.
- Spec-less edge probes AI-06 and AI-08 came back unclassified («review manually»): flagged here as reviewed by hand. AI-06 (merge never overwrites, persists nothing) is covered by the 03-01 truths and this plan's merge truths; AI-08 by the missing-list truths here and the highlight truths of plan 03-05 (which also takes AI-07). No probe item is dismissed.
- AI-02 precision is lifted here for the web (String(n) into the form); the API part is in 03-03.
