## Phase Goal

**As a** job seeker, **I want to** create, find, review and move my applications through their states, **so that** I have one reliable record of every selection process.

This plan opens an application from its card into the full read-only detail. Plan 02-07 (wave 5) serves it from the API.

<objective>
Let the user open an application from a card into a detail that shows offer data, grouped tags, notes, the collapsible original text and the status history, with no rounds or documents sections (D-13, D-15, APP-09).

APP-09 is delivered **partially** in Phase 2: every clause except «linked documents». That clause is owned by Phase 5 (DOC-03, DOC-04), and the interview rounds section by Phase 4 (INT-05), as ROADMAP Cross-Phase Notes record. This plan must not claim APP-09 complete.

Purpose: the detail is where status changes and deletes happen (plan 02-09) and where the user lands after saving the form (plans 02-10, 02-12).
Output: route, scene and complete application-detail pod with its sections and states; card link.
</objective>
